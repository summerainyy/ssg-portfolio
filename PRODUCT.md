## Platform

web

## Users
OJT supervisors and HR staff at IT companies in the Philippines reviewing applicants for an IT on-the-job training slot. They skim quickly, often on a laptop between other applications, and want to know within seconds who Summer is, what she can build, and how to reach her.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Summer Anne S. San Gabriel, a 4th-year BS Information Technology student (major in System Development) at Central Luzon State University, graduating 2027. Success means an OJT supervisor understands her skills and projects and contacts her (email, phone or Facebook) or downloads her résumé.

## Positioning
A front-end developer in training who also writes the technical documentation that holds a system together, and brings real visual craft (UI/UX, graphic design, photography, video editing) to her work. The photography on the site is her own world, not stock.

## Operating Context
Static single page (HTML, CSS, vanilla JS), deployed on Render (`render.yaml`). The site mark is her initials, "SSG". Visitors arrive from a résumé, application email or Facebook link. The Facebook link is labeled with her full name, "Summer Anne San Gabriel".

## Product Principles
1. A busy supervisor gets name, role, availability and a way to contact within the first viewport.
2. Show the work: real project images and specifics, not claims.

4. Her photography carries the personality; the layout lets it lead.
